# Acme Coffee brand guideline

Acme Coffee is energetic, optimistic, modern, and approachable. The brand speaks clearly and
confidently without sounding arrogant or overly casual. Vietnamese copy should be concise,
friendly, and action-oriented.

The primary visual colors are deep charcoal `#101820`, citrus orange `#F4A261`, and warm cream
`#FFF4E6`. Posters should use bold geometric sans-serif typography, generous negative space, and
authentic product photography. The product must remain the visual hero.

Always include the approved Acme Coffee logo and an accurate product image. Do not distort the
logo, place it on low-contrast backgrounds, or recolor it outside the approved palette.

Never invent prices, awards, certifications, health benefits, medical effects, sustainability
claims, or performance statistics. Product claims must be supported by approved product material.

The core audience is urban professionals aged 22–35 who want a refreshing coffee experience with
clear flavor information and low-friction ordering.
