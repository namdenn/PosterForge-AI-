# Acme Citrus Cold Brew approved product facts

Acme Citrus Cold Brew combines cold-brew coffee with a light citrus aroma. The approved campaign
description is: “Cold brew vị cam nhẹ dành cho người trẻ làm việc tại thành phố.”

The campaign may describe the taste as refreshing, citrus-forward, smooth, and lightly sweet. It
must not claim medical, nutritional, or therapeutic benefits. No price has been approved for this
campaign, so generated materials must not contain a price or discount percentage.

Approved mandatory poster elements are the Acme Coffee logo and an accurate image of the product.
